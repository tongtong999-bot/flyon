Cookie Policy

Learn how we use cookies and tracking technologies to improve your experience.

Last Updated: March 25, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Galiview Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website. By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of cookies in accordance with this policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

Cookies allow websites to remember your actions and preferences over time, so you don't have to keep re-entering them whenever you come back to the site or browse from page to page.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility. Without these cookies, the website cannot function properly.

  • Authentication and security
  • Shopping cart functionality
  • Form validation and fraud prevention

2.2 Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous information about their usage patterns. This data helps us improve the website's performance and user experience.

  • Page views and user sessions
  • Time spent on pages
  • Error reporting and debugging
  • Performance monitoring

2.3 Functionality Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as remembering your preferences, language settings, and location.

  • Remembering your preferences
  • Language selection
  • Font size adjustments
  • Dark mode preferences

2.4 Targeting and Advertising Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver advertisements that are relevant to you and your interests. They may be used by third-party advertising networks to track your browsing history across different websites.

  • Personalized advertisements
  • Market research
  • Analytics and reporting

3. Third-Party Cookies

We may allow third-party services to place cookies on your device through our website. These third parties may include:

  • Analytics Services: Tools like Google Analytics help us understand user behavior and improve our services
  • Social Media Platforms: Buttons and widgets that allow you to share content on social media
  • Payment Processors: Secure payment gateways for online transactions
  • Advertising Networks: Services that display relevant advertisements

These third parties have their own privacy policies and may use their cookies for their own purposes. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

4. How We Use Your Information

The information collected by cookies may be used for the following purposes:

  • To provide a personalized experience
  • To improve website functionality and performance
  • To analyze usage patterns and optimize content
  • To deliver relevant advertisements and content
  • To enhance security and prevent fraudulent activity
  • To comply with legal requirements

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can control and manage cookies in various ways:

5.1 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject all cookies
  • Delete cookies that have been set
  • Set cookie preferences by type or by website

5.2 Browser-Specific Instructions

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Website tracking
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

5.3 Cookie Consent Banner

Our website may display a cookie consent banner that allows you to choose which types of cookies you consent to. Your preferences will be saved and respected in accordance with applicable laws.

6. Impact of Disabling Cookies

Please be aware that disabling cookies may impact the functionality of our website. Some features may not work as intended, or you may experience reduced functionality and a less personalized experience. For example:

  • You may not be able to complete online purchases
  • Preferences and settings may not be saved between sessions
  • Some interactive features may be unavailable
  • The website may load more slowly

7. Cookies and Privacy Laws

7.1 GDPR (EU)

If you are located in the European Union, we require your explicit consent for non-essential cookies. You can withdraw your consent at any time through your browser settings or our cookie management tool.

7.2 CCPA (California)

California residents have the right to opt out of the sale of their personal information, including the use of cookies for targeted advertising. You can exercise this right through our privacy settings.
