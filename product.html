Technical Details

Complete Specifications

Every detail designed for professional performance and ease of use.

📷 Camera System

Camera: 1/2.3-inch CMOS

Effective Pixels: 48 megapixels

Video: 4K/60fps, 2.7K/60fps, 1080p/120fps

Photo Modes: Single, Burst, AEB, Timed, HDR

ISO Range: 100-3200 (auto), 100-1600 (manual)

Aperture: f/2.8, fixed focus

🎯 Gimbal System

Type: 3-axis mechanical gimbal

Stabilization: ±0.01°

Controllable Range: Pitch: -90° to +40°

Max Speed: 120°/s

Vibration Reduction: Advanced electronic shake reduction

✈️ Flight Performance

Max Flight Time: 25 minutes

Max Speed: 16 m/s (35.8 mph) in Sport mode

Max Altitude: 5000 m (above sea level)

Wind Resistance: Level 5 (8-10.7 m/s)

Propulsion: Brushless motors, self-tightening props

📡 Transmission System

Technology: OcuSync 2.0 (custom)

Max Range: 10 km (CE), 6 km (FCC)

Live View Quality: 720p / 30fps

Latency: 120-130ms

Frequency: 2.400-2.4835 GHz, 5.725-5.850 GHz

🛡️ Obstacle Sensing

Forward: Visual sensors, detection range 0.5-12 m

Backward: Visual sensors, detection range 0.5-12 m

Downward: Dual vision sensors + infrared, <12 m

Upward: Visual sensors, 0.5-12 m

Active Avoidance: Intelligent flight path planning

🔋 Battery System

Capacity: 3850 mAh

Voltage: 11.55 V

Type: LiPo 3S

Charging Temperature: 5°C to 40°C

Charger Power: 18 W (included)

Intelligent Features

Advanced Capabilities

Smart features designed to help you capture stunning footage effortlessly.

🎯 ActiveTrack 3.0

Advanced subject recognition and tracking maintains perfect framing on moving subjects, including people, vehicles, and animals, even in complex environments.

🎬 QuickShot Modes

One-tap cinematic shots including Dronie, Helix, Rocket, Circle, and Boomerang automatically create professional-quality video sequences.

📸 Smart Photo Features

FocusTrack, Hyperlapse, Mastershots, and automatic scene recognition ensure you always get the perfect shot with minimal effort.

Intelligent RTH automatically returns the drone to its takeoff point on low battery, lost connection, or manual activation with obstacle avoidance.

Simple hand gestures control photo capture and video recording, perfect for solo adventures or group shots without a remote controller.

Customize flight paths by setting waypoints, altitude, and speed for autonomous cinematic footage perfect for mapping, surveying, or creative storytelling.
