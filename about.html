About Galiview

See Beyond Limits — Empowering creators with innovative aerial technology.

Our Vision & Mission

To democratize aerial photography and videography by making professional-grade drone technology accessible, intuitive, and reliable for everyone. — See Beyond Limits.

Our Story

Galiview was founded in 2022 by a team of passionate engineers and photography enthusiasts who shared a common vision: to bridge the gap between professional aerial imaging and everyday creativity. Based in the heart of San Francisco's technology innovation hub, we've been pushing the boundaries of what's possible in consumer drone technology.

Our journey began with a simple question: Why should professional-grade aerial photography require professional-grade budgets? This question drove us to develop innovative solutions that bring sophisticated camera systems, intelligent flight modes, and user-friendly operation to creators of all skill levels.

Our Values

  • Innovation: We continuously push technological boundaries to deliver cutting-edge features and performance.
  • Accessibility: We believe professional-grade tools should be available and approachable for everyone.
  • Quality: Every Galiview product undergoes rigorous testing to ensure reliability and exceptional performance.
  • Safety: We prioritize user and public safety through intelligent design and comprehensive obstacle avoidance systems.
  • Sustainability: We're committed to reducing our environmental footprint through eco-friendly materials and responsible manufacturing.

What We Believe

We believe that when you remove barriers to creativity, amazing things happen. When a photographer can capture a perspective that was previously out of reach, when a filmmaker can add aerial sequences to their story without breaking their budget, when an enthusiast can explore their world from above — that's when innovation truly matters.

Our products are designed not just with features and specifications, but with the understanding that every flight, every shot, every moment captured is someone's creative vision coming to life.
